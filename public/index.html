About the Competition

What is VSPARK and what can you expect?

What to Expect

Each accepted team receives a dedicated 20-minute competition window on the day. You will see a scenario and instructions. Generate an original AI video and submit a Google Drive link containing your video and the prompts you used.

Judges will evaluate prompt engineering quality, scenario accuracy, visual storytelling and creativity. Their decision is final.

Rules

  • Bring your own laptop, tool accounts and credits.
  • Runway, Kling, Pika, Sora, CapCut, Flow and similar tools are allowed.
  • AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude may help with prompts.
  • Videos must be created during the 20 minutes. Pre-made videos are not accepted.
  • Don't copy another team's prompts or fabricate generation evidence.
  • Judges may inspect your prompts. Their decision is final.
